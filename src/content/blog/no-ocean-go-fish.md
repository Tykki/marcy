Did you know a vast majority of the Earth’s ocean coasts are controlled by a handful of countries? Andrew Sharpless, CEO and keynote speaker for day two of this year’s Fashinnovation claims these countries can work together to improve the health of the ocean by cleaning their portion of the coast. Sharpless explains in his segment the importance of the preservation of the ocean as it has human health benefits and can resolve other serious world issues.

For those who hate fish, improving the quality of fish for other people still offers health benefits. Sharpless explains eating fish is healthier than red meat and can lower one’s risk for diabetes, heart disease, and cancer. In addition, a better ocean with even better fish leaves a larger supply of fresh clean water for people to drink. He asserts being able to rely on the ocean as a larger supplier of food creates less greenhouse gas by minimizing agriculture related pollution such as life stock production. Less air and water pollution can aid individuals with preexisting conditions and compromised immune systems.

How can one help? Supporting antipollution and anti-overfishing efforts can improve the health of the ocean and increase the amount of healthy fish. Sharpless asserts transparency will continue to aid the various efforts Oceana and other organizations are piloting. By staying involved and being aware of the food production practices, the overall quality and care that is put into the acquisition of fish will improve. What to know more? Visit Oceana.org/fashinnovation for more information on how you can help improve the health of the ocean.
